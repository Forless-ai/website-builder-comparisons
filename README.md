## How We Compare Website Builders

The lowest advertised price does not always represent the cost of a professional business website.

This research therefore considers:

- Published paid-plan pricing
- Custom-domain support
- Hosting
- SSL/security
- AI website creation
- Editing and customization
- Business functionality
- Intended use case
- Promotional versus renewal pricing

Free plans are treated separately when they require a platform subdomain or restrict features normally expected from a professional business website.
